Formatting Modes

  • Format Entire Text: Converts all typed or pasted characters immediately. Great for social media posts, bios, and headlines.
  • Marked Text: Only text wrapped in the chosen delimiter (e.g. **bold**) is converted. Everything outside delimiters remains normal text.

Offline & Privacy

100% of formatting happens locally in your browser. No text is sent to any server. This app functions entirely offline.
