How Formatting Works

  • Format Everything: Simply type or paste your text. Everything converts to the selected Unicode style automatically.
  • Format Specific Words: Wrap words with markers like **bold**. Only marked sections will be formatted; the rest stays as plain text.

Offline & Privacy

100% of formatting happens locally in your browser. No text is sent to any server. This app functions entirely offline.
